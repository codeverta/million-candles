## The Art of Candle Care: Ensuring Longevity and Safety

Once you've found the perfect candle, it's important to take proper care of it to ensure its longevity and safety. Here are a few tips:

- **Trim the wick:** Before each burn, trim the wick to ¼ inch. This will prevent smoking and ensure a clean, even burn.
- **Burn for at least 2 hours:** Allow the candle to burn for at least 2 hours during the first burn to create a full melt pool. This will prevent tunneling and ensure that the candle burns evenly down.
- **Keep away from drafts:** Avoid burning candles in drafty areas, as this can cause uneven burning and flickering.
- **Never leave a burning candle unattended:** Always extinguish a candle before leaving the room or going to sleep.
- **Place on a heat-resistant surface:** Always place candles on a heat-resistant surface to prevent damage to furniture.
- **Keep away from flammable materials:** Keep candles away from flammable materials, such as curtains, paper, and clothing.
- **Store properly:** Store candles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

By following these simple tips, you can extend the life of your candles and enjoy their fragrance and ambiance safely.

## FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

**1. What is the best type of wax for candles?**

The "best" type of wax depends on your preferences. Soy wax is a popular choice for its natural properties, clean burn, and ability to hold fragrance well. Beeswax is another natural option known for its subtle honey scent and long burn time. Paraffin wax is a more affordable option, but it's not as environmentally friendly.

**2. How do I prevent my candle from tunneling?**

Ensure the first burn creates a full melt pool (wax melted evenly to the edge of the jar). This "memory ring" will help the candle burn evenly in subsequent uses.
