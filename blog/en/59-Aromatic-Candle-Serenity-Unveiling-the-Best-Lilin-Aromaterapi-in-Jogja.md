- **Q: What is the best type of wax for aromatic candles?**

  - **A:** Soy wax and beeswax are generally considered the best choices due to their natural origins, clean burn, and ability to hold fragrance well.

- **Q: How long should I burn an aromatic candle?**

  - **A:** For the first burn, allow the candle to burn long enough for the entire top layer of wax to melt evenly. Subsequent burns should last for 1-4 hours.

- **Q: How do I care for my aromatic candle?**

  - **A:** Trim the wick to ¼ inch before each burn to prevent smoking and ensure an even burn. Avoid burning candles in drafty areas.

- **Q: Are aromatic candles safe for pets?**

  - **A:** Some essential oils can be harmful to pets. Avoid using candles with strong scents or those containing oils that are known to be toxic to animals. Always burn candles in a well-ventilated area and keep them out of reach of pets.
