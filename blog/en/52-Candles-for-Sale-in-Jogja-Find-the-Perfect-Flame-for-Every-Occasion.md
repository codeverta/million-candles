- **Beeswax Candles:** Made from beeswax, these candles are another natural and sustainable option. They have a subtle honey scent and burn with a bright, warm flame. Beeswax candles are also known for purifying the air.

## Factors to Consider When Buying Candles in Jogja

Choosing the right candle can be a delightful experience, but it's essential to consider a few factors to ensure you get the best value for your money and choose a candle that meets your needs.

### Wax Type

- **Paraffin Wax:** This is the most common type of wax used in candles. It's affordable and holds fragrance well. However, paraffin wax is a petroleum byproduct and can release soot into the air when burned.

- **Soy Wax:** Soy wax is a natural and renewable alternative to paraffin wax. It burns cleaner, longer, and releases less soot. Soy candles are also biodegradable and environmentally friendly.

- **Beeswax:** Beeswax is another natural and sustainable option. It has a subtle honey scent and burns with a bright, warm flame. Beeswax candles are also known for purifying the air.

- **Palm Wax:** Palm wax is derived from palm oil. It's a hard wax that holds fragrance well and creates a crystalline effect when burned. However, the sustainability of palm wax production is a concern.

### Fragrance

- **Essential Oils:** Candles scented with essential oils offer therapeutic benefits and a more natural fragrance.

- **Fragrance Oils:** Fragrance oils are synthetic fragrances that are often more affordable and offer a wider range of scents.

- **Scent Throw:** Consider the scent throw of the candle, which refers to how well the fragrance fills a room. A stronger scent throw is desirable for larger spaces, while a more subtle scent is better for smaller rooms.

### Burn Time

The burn time of a candle depends on the type of wax, the size of the candle, and the quality of the wick. Check the product label for the estimated burn time before purchasing.

### Wick Type

- **Cotton Wicks:** These are the most common type of wick. They are made from natural fibers and provide a clean and consistent burn.

- **Wood Wicks:** Wood wicks create a crackling sound when burned, adding to the ambiance. They also tend to burn slower and more evenly.
