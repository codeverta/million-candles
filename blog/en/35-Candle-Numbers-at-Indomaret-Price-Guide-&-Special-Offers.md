### Beyond Price: Quality and Safety Considerations

While price is an important factor, it's crucial to consider the quality and safety of number candles.

- **Material Safety:** Ensure that the candles are made from non-toxic materials. Avoid candles with excessive amounts of glitter or other potentially hazardous decorations.
- **Burning Time:** Consider the burning time of the candles. You want candles that will burn long enough for the birthday song and photo opportunities but not so long that they become a fire hazard.
- **Stability:** Choose candles that are stable and won't easily tip over when placed on the cake.
- **Flame Control:** Ensure that the candles produce a consistent and controllable flame. Avoid candles that flicker excessively or emit sparks.

Always supervise children around lit candles and ensure that they are extinguished properly after use.

### Practical Tips for Using Number Candles

- **Choose the Right Size:** Select number candles that are appropriately sized for the cake. Too small, and they might get lost in the frosting. Too large, and they might overpower the cake.
- **Secure the Candles:** Ensure that the candles are securely inserted into the cake. Use a toothpick or candle holder if necessary.
- **Space the Candles:** Space the candles evenly to prevent them from burning too close together.
- **Supervise Children:** Never leave children unattended around lit candles.
- **Extinguish Properly:** Use a candle snuffer or gently blow out the candles. Avoid using water, as it can cause the wax to splatter.
- **Take Photos Quickly:** Have your camera ready to capture the moment when the candles are lit and the birthday song is sung.
- **Dispose of Carefully:** Discard used candles properly. Allow them to cool completely before disposing of them in the trash.
